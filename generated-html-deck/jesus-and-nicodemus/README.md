# Jesus and Nicodemus - HTML teaching deck

Offline-friendly 16:9 teaching deck for The Gospel Project: Kids, Unit 21, Session 3.

## Lesson source

- Lifeway Ministry Grid session: Jesus and Nicodemus
- Reviewed: 22 October 2026
- Bible passage: John 3
- Key passage: 1 John 3:5, CSB wording
- Content reviewed: Younger Kids and Older Kids Leader Guides, Bible Story Picture, Big Picture Question and Answer, Story Point, and Key Passage Posters.
- Main artwork: the local Lifeway Bible Story Picture in assets/jesus-and-nicodemus-story.jpg.
- The session page also offers videos and audio. They are not embedded, keeping this pack small and usable offline.

## Teaching plan

Slides 1-8 form a 10-minute teaching block. Their pacing notes total 10 minutes. Slide 9 is a separate 5-minute discussion with four tap-to-reveal answer cues. Slide 10 ends with a 30-second read-aloud prayer.

The deck covers Nicodemus' visit, Jesus' teaching about new life from the Spirit, the cross, God's love, the story point, and the big-picture answer. It corrects the ideas that being born again means returning to babyhood or that religious status and good behavior earn salvation.

## Open and navigate

Open index.html in a browser. For a local web server, run python3 -m http.server 8000 from this folder, then open http://localhost:8000/.

Use the First, Previous, Next, and Last buttons, or the Left Arrow, Right Arrow, Space, Home, and End keys. Tap or click the question bubbles; focused buttons also respond to Enter and Space.

Direct slide links use the hash format #slide=6. The deck clamps invalid slide numbers and supports browser back and forward.

Presenter notes are off by default. Append ?presenter=1 to the URL to show per-slide pacing, Scripture references, and leader notes below the TV-facing stage.
