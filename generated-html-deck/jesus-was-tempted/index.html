A hard beginning

40 days in the wilderness

Right after His baptism, the Holy Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil.

40
No food. No crowd.When the 40 days ended, Jesus was very hungry.
Jesus was truly human. He felt hunger and faced a real test.
After His baptism

Matthew 4:1-2 · Mark 1:12-13 · Luke 4:1-2

Temptation 1
The first test

“Turn stones into bread.”

Satan questioned Jesus’ identity and offered an easy fix for His hunger.

“Man must not live on bread alone.”Jesus answered with God’s Word.
He trusted His Father instead of using His power for Himself.

Matthew 4:3-4 · Deuteronomy 8:3

The second test

“Bow down and worship.”

Satan offered Jesus all the kingdoms of the world if Jesus would worship him.

“Worship the Lord your God, and serve Him only.”God alone deserves our worship.
Jesus chose worship and obedience to God alone.
Temptation 2

Matthew 4:8-10 · Deuteronomy 6:13

Temptation 3
The third test

“Jump. Make God prove it.”

Satan twisted Scripture and dared Jesus to jump from the temple.

“Do not test the Lord your God.”God is trustworthy; we do not manipulate Him.
Jesus refused to force God to prove His care.

Matthew 4:5-7 · Deuteronomy 6:16

The turning point

Jesus stood firm.

The story point

Jesus was tempted and never sinned.

God’s Word is true—even when lies sound clever.
Jesus is the only perfect Savior. He overcame every temptation.

He understands
Why this is good news

Jesus understands temptation.

He became human and felt what temptation is like. He also remained perfectly obedient.

Like usJesus experienced hunger, testing, and hardship.
Unlike usJesus never sinned.
For usHis perfect life is credited to everyone who trusts Him.
Our hopeWe are saved by grace through faith—not by trying to earn it.
No. Jesus is the perfect Savior. We trust Him.

Christ Connection · Matthew 4; Mark 1; Luke 4

Remember who Jesus is

God the Son came to live among us.

Is Jesus God or a human?
Jesus is fully God and fully human.

“The Word became flesh and dwelt among us ... full of grace and truth.” — John 1:14

Jesus was really human, so He knows temptation. Jesus is really God, so He is the only sinless Savior.

Jesus was tempted, but He never sinned—and He can save us.
