# Jesus Was Tempted

An offline-friendly, 16:9 teaching deck for The Gospel Project for Kids, Unit 20, Session 3. It combines the Older Kids and Younger Kids leader guides into a short story flow for a mixed small group.

## Source and content

- Ministry Grid session: [Jesus Was Tempted](https://ministrygrid.lifeway.com/#/curriculum/TGP/Kids/session/0fd1ffd2-981f-4c09-93aa-f897eff42258/issue/236d965f-66a1-481e-a729-c854a3c3369c)
- Leader resources reviewed: Older Kids Leader Guide and Younger Kids Leader Guide, Unit 20, Session 3.
- Poster resources reviewed: Bible Story Picture, Big Picture Question and Answer, Key Passage Posters, and Story Point.
- Bible passage: Matthew 4; Mark 1; Luke 4.
- Big Picture Question: “Is Jesus God or a human?” Answer: “Jesus is fully God and fully human.”
- Story Point: “Jesus was tempted and never sinned.”
- Key passage: John 1:14. The deck uses a short CSB excerpt; read the full verse from the group’s Bible.
- Local artwork: the illustrated temple scene and poster crops in `assets/`, rasterized from the Lifeway PDFs reviewed for this session (© 2025 Lifeway).

The source Bible Story Video, Questions from Kids Video, and activity pages are intentionally omitted. The deck makes no external requests after its local files are present.

## Open and navigate

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit `/generated-html-deck/jesus-was-tempted/`.

- Use `←` / `→` for previous and next, `Home` / `End` for first and last, and `Space` for next when a button is not focused. The on-screen navigation buttons also support click and touch.
- Jump directly to a slide with `#slide=1` through `#slide=9`; invalid values are clamped. Browser back and forward follow slide history.
- Presenter notes are off by default. Add `?presenter=1` to the URL or press `P` to show them; press `P` or `Escape` to hide them.
- On the final slide, tap/click or keyboard-activate a question button to reveal its answer cue.

## Teaching flow

Eight story and memory slides pace the teaching for about 10 minutes. The final slide holds four discussion prompts for the separate 5-minute question period. The presentation distinguishes Jesus’ real human temptation from sin and keeps salvation grounded in Jesus’ perfect obedience and grace through faith.
