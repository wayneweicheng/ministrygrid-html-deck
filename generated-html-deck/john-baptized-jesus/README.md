# John Baptized Jesus

An offline-friendly, 16:9 teaching deck for The Gospel Project for Kids, Unit 20, Session 2. It is planned for a small mixed or unspecified-age group; the slide language is younger-kid friendly, with the older-kid theological extension kept in presenter notes.

## Source and content

- Ministry Grid session: [John Baptized Jesus](https://ministrygrid.lifeway.com/#/curriculum/TGP/Kids/session/92435482-a6a5-426a-b186-ae29bc666189/issue/236d965f-66a1-481e-a729-c854a3c3369c)
- Leader resources reviewed: Older Kids Leader Guide and Younger Kids Leader Guide, Unit 20, Session 2.
- Poster resources reviewed: Bible Story Picture, Big Picture Question and Answer, Key Passage Posters, and Story Point.
- Bible passage: Matthew 3; Mark 1; Luke 3; John 1.
- Big Picture Question: “Is Jesus God or a human?” Answer: “Jesus is fully God and fully human.”
- Story Point: “Jesus obeyed God by being baptized.”
- Key passage: John 1:14. The deck shows a short CSB excerpt; read the full verse from the group’s Bible.
- Source story artwork: `assets/john-baptized-jesus-story.png`, rasterized from the Lifeway Bible Story Picture PDF (© 2025 Lifeway).

The source Bible Story Video is listed on Ministry Grid but is not included. The deck makes no external requests and works offline once its local image is present.

## Open and navigate

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit `/generated-html-deck/john-baptized-jesus/`.

- Use `←` / `→` for previous and next, `Home` / `End` for first and last, and `Space` for next when a button is not focused. The on-screen navigation buttons also support click and touch.
- Jump directly to a slide with `#slide=1` through `#slide=9`; invalid values are clamped. Browser back and forward follow slide history.
- Presenter notes are off by default. Add `?presenter=1` to the URL or press `P` to show them; press `P` or `Escape` to hide them.
- On the final slide, tap/click or keyboard-activate a question button to reveal its answer cue.

## Teaching flow

Eight story and memory slides pace the teaching for about 10 minutes. The final slide holds four discussion prompts for the separate 5-minute question period. The presentation distinguishes Jesus’ sinless obedience from baptism for forgiveness and explains that baptism is a sign of faith, not what saves.
